Students. Engineers. Teammates. We turn shared ambition into something we can take to the track.

A workshop. A proving ground.

THE CAR IS
ONLY THE
BEGINNING.

Established in 2009, our team story describes 60 students, primarily from Mechanical Engineering.

Each year, we design, manufacture and compete with a formula-style race car. Along the way we learn to connect technical execution with structured business and project management.

Our mission is to develop industry-ready engineers. Alumni have progressed into high-performance motorsport, including Formula 1, engineering consultancy and diverse engineering sectors.
