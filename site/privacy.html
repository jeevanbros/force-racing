Data Transparency on Pit Lane

Force Racing is a student-run Formula Student engineering team at Kakatiya Institute of Technology & Science, Warangal. We stand for technical integrity, direct accountability, and genuine respect for your personal information. Here is our straightforward, honest data commitment.

1. What We Collect

When you register for F1 Sim Racing or submit a sponsorship proposal, we collect only the necessary details you explicitly enter:

  • Your Name & Email Address: To generate your official Pilot Pass ID and dispatch your scannable QR credential.
  • Mobile Number: For immediate trackside SMS/WhatsApp check-in at the Central Workshop pit lane.
  • College / Institution or Organization: To verify student admission or corporate partnership status.
  • Payment Screenshot: To verify your ₹99 entry fee trackside so you can enter the racing simulator cockpit without delay.

2. No Trackers, No Cookies, No Data Selling

We do not use tracking pixels, invasive analytics, third-party advertising cookies, or behavioral trackers. We will NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your contact details to third-party marketing companies.

3. Where Your Information is Stored

Your details are stored securely in:

  • Our team's private, encrypted Cloudflare database (D1).
  • Our master event Google Sheet on our official KITS Warangal Google Drive workspace.
  • Payment screenshots are stored in a dedicated, restricted Google Drive folder ("Force Racing Payment Proofs") accessible only to authorized student organizers (Md. Sohail, S. Arun, Dhanush).

4. Immediate Deletion Upon Request

You have full ownership of your data. If you want your registration record or payment proof permanently erased from our records, email us at team.force.racing.kitsw@gmail.com with your Pass ID, and we will purge your row within 24 hours.
