Data Transparency on Pit Lane

Force Racing is a student-run Formula Student engineering team at Kakatiya Institute of Technology & Science, Warangal. We stand for technical integrity, direct accountability, and genuine respect for your personal information. Here is our straightforward, honest data commitment.

1. What We Collect

When you submit a sponsorship proposal, recruitment application or contact enquiry, we collect only the necessary details you explicitly enter:

  • Your Name & Email Address: To respond to your enquiry or application and keep you updated on our progress.
  • Mobile Number: So the team can follow up on your enquiry.
  • College / Institution or Organization: To verify student admission or corporate partnership status.

2. No Trackers, No Cookies, No Data Selling

We do not use tracking pixels, invasive analytics, third-party advertising cookies, or behavioral trackers. We will NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your contact details to third-party marketing companies.

3. Where Your Information is Stored

Your details are stored securely in:

  • Our team's private, encrypted Cloudflare database (D1).
  • Our official KITS Warangal Google Drive workspace.

4. Immediate Deletion Upon Request

You have full ownership of your data. If you want your enquiry or application record permanently erased from our records, email us at team.force.racing.kitsw@gmail.com with your team email address, and we will purge your row within 24 hours.
