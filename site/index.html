We are the Formula Student team of Kakatiya Institute of Technology & Science, Warangal.

Established in 2009, Force Racing brings students from the classroom to the workshop. We design, manufacture and compete with formula-style race cars in national and international Formula Student competitions.

Our alumni have progressed into high-performance motorsport, including Formula 1, engineering consultancy and other engineering sectors.
