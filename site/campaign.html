Before you join us

A FEW
ANSWERS.

When and where is the event?

F1 Sim Racing is on 31 October 2026 at the Central Workshop, Kakatiya Institute of Technology & Science, Warangal. Contact the organizers before planning your visit.

How much does registration cost?

The official registration fee is ₹99 per participant. You can pay via UPI or at the Central Workshop desk. A digital scannable QR Pilot Credential will be dispatched to your email immediately upon submission.

How do I register?

Complete the on-site registration form above to get your official Pilot Pass ID and QR code, or use the alternative Google Form linked from the event poster.

Does the Google Form require sign-in?

Yes. The registration link from the poster redirects signed-out visitors to Google sign-in. Open it in your browser and sign in to continue.

Are game details, timings or prizes confirmed?

Contact student organizer Md. Sohail on +91 80083 91525 for specific simulation slot schedules, hardware telemetry, and track regulations.

Who is presenting the event?

The Department of Mechanical Engineering at Kakatiya Institute of Technology & Science, Warangal, presents the event under SUMSHODHINI ’26.
