# Category-aware art direction

Krishna's standing preference, 6 October 2026: deliver a distinctive, fully composed
website on the first serious preview. ManaMela's fashion editorial design is an
approved quality reference. Carry its care and visual judgement into every category;
choose a new visual language for each business. Do not wait for Krishna to repeat
"premium", "beautiful photos", "better fonts", or "check mobile".

## Decide the direction before assembling components

Read the actual business, audience, customer task, existing identity and usable
assets. In the client's DESIGN.md record the chosen visual direction and why it
fits; briefly consider a materially different alternative when the direction is
open. This is an internal design decision, not a mandatory owner questionnaire.
Existing approved direction takes precedence over exploring another redesign.

Define together: hero composition, type contrast, image subject and crop, palette,
graphic vocabulary, section rhythm, action hierarchy, mobile composition and motion.
A hero, product grid, forms and footer must belong to the same system. Layout must
respond to the real photos and content. Avoid making interchangeable card grids the
starting point for every business. A custom element earns its place by expressing
the brand or explaining something a visitor needs.

## Category cues and useful alternatives

These guide selection; they are not obligatory templates or permission to add features.

| Business / customer task | Suitable directions and visual evidence | Useful information and action |
|---|---|---|
| Fashion, jewellery / choose a piece | Fashion editorial, refined catalogue, expressive contemporary; accurate product photography and authorised model styling; strong type hierarchy, deliberate wardrobe/colour relationships | Product detail, gallery, factual price, availability and actual ordering route; filters only when justified |
| General retail, industrial stock / find the correct item | Clean catalogue, technical clarity, warm neighbourhood identity; exact stock photos and useful specifications | Category navigation, fit/compatibility when evidenced, quote/enquiry or real purchase route |
| Healthcare / choose care and reach the provider | Calm editorial, approachable local, precise clinical; real staff/premises and legible information | Verified services and credentials, location, actual appointment/contact flow; never fictional outcomes or credentials |
| Food, stays, travel / understand the experience | Atmospheric editorial, lively local, restrained luxury; truthful food, rooms and location photography | Menu/rooms, real rates if authorised, directions, booking/enquiry; no generated food or premises presented as real |
| Professional services / assess expertise | Typographic authority, human portfolio, structured case studies; authorised portraits and verifiable work | Services, process, evidenced case studies and a clear contact task |
| Software product / understand the capability | Product-led editorial, functional minimalism, explanatory diagrams; actual UI or clearly labelled concept visuals | Real feature demonstration, documentation and implemented trial/contact route; no fake integrations or success states |
| Other category | Derive from its audience, evidence and buying/usage task; identify fitting established design families | Include only the information and mechanics the business can support |

Choose button weight and motion from the hierarchy. ManaMela's outlined commerce
buttons are its approved preference, not a universal ban on filled buttons.
Likewise a serif, full-bleed model, specific flower or colour belongs to that client.

## Images are designed with the layout

- Use the image skill/provider appropriate to the requested medium when generation
  or editing is authorised. Keep exact prompts, reference roles, provenance, originals
  and review candidates. Plan subject, composition, crop and negative space together.
- Separate visual direction from factual evidence. A generated model/photo cannot
  prove product construction, material, dimensions, premises, staff or results.
- With authorised brand models, use their identity cards and relevant masters. Lock
  facial identity, skin tone, anatomy and product detail. Catalogue viewing direction,
  worn scale, wardrobe and headroom should be intentionally consistent where required.
- Premium lighting means faithful material texture, controlled highlights and useful
  separation. Never upgrade jewellery into a different product or misstate its material.
- Inspect the photograph alone and rendered in the page. No wash, gradient, typography
  or graphic may accidentally obscure the face, product, food or other key subject.
  Place copy in negative space, narrow the overlay, or recompose the layout. Review
  mobile crops independently; a desktop crop is not automatically a mobile composition.
- Concept illustration may explain or decorate, clearly separated from evidence of
  the business. Never generate pretend premises, staff, testimonials or stock.

## Finish and proof

Carry the identity through the mark, wordmark, favicon, headings, image captions,
controls, gallery, empty states and footer. Prefer space, alignment and meaningful
contrast; use glass only where transparency is useful. Motion should have a visible
purpose and a reduced-motion alternative. It must never gate access to content.

Run the existing checks, then inspect actual browser screenshots of the opening,
middle and footer, plus key secondary pages, at mobile and desktop sizes and all
supported themes. Assess subject clarity, crop, type collisions, CTA hierarchy,
colour fidelity, touch usability and section rhythm. Correct visible defects and
recapture the affected views. Do not equate a clean build or axe result with good art
direction. Do not weaken accessibility checks to preserve tiny editorial labels.

Save one design specification, exact token values, image provenance, font licences,
and reusable brand exports with the client. Preserve the established stack and
source/generated boundary. Provide a small asset index so the owner can find the
logo, fonts and selected images without reading implementation code.
